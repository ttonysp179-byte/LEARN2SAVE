About Me

I’m Anthony, a budgeting coach who speaks in dollars, not confusing jargon.

I work mainly with 18–20 year olds who are just starting out with money: first jobs, first bank accounts, and zero formal financial education. My goal is to make money feel simple, clear, and doable—no judgment, no lectures, just real conversations and practical plans.
